# Stillpage — Project goal

Stillpage should feel like a quiet, dependable place to quickly save things you want to read or watch later.

Saving and finding something again should be fast, with as little waiting and friction as possible. People should feel that their links are safely out of the noisy feed and easy to return to. The app should help them focus on the content rather than the platform it came from. It should feel calm, clear, and respectful of their time—not like another feed competing for attention.

The experience should be fast and dependable without pretending the web is predictable. The app should respond quickly, and avoid making people wait on work that can happen later. Some links will not load or be available in full; the app should be honest about that and make it easy to continue to the original source.

Stillpage should feel personal and comfortable for different reading preferences, while remaining simple to use. It should earn trust by keeping the user's saved collection theirs and by making the basic experience useful without unnecessary setup.

Use this goal to guide product and design choices. It describes the intended feeling, not a feature checklist or implementation plan. Use specific requirements and platform constraints to decide what to build.
